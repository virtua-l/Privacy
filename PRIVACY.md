# VIRTUAL Client — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 25, 2026_

VIRTUAL Client ("the Mod") does not collect, store, or sell personal data, and it has no servers or analytics.

## What stays on your computer
Your settings (menu position, theme, HUD layout, and the optional Discord Application ID) are saved locally in `config/virtual-client.json`. This file is never uploaded by the Mod.

## Discord Rich Presence
If you enable Rich Presence, the Mod sends activity text (for example, that you are playing Minecraft) to the Discord app running on your own computer. Discord then displays it on your profile according to your Discord privacy settings. Discord's own policy applies to that data: https://discord.com/privacy

## Minecraft account information
The Mod reads your in-game username and skin only to display them locally in its own menus. It does not transmit them anywhere.
